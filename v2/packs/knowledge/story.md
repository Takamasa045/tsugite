# Story planning

State the audience, purpose and final action before proposing shots. Give each
shot one readable idea and explain what changes from the preceding shot. Show
the main subject early, keep source timing explicit, and leave space for text.
For recurring characters, confirm appearance, voice, behavior and location in
plain language before selecting generation tasks. Reuse approved local identity
references; a guide is advice, not authority to generate or approve anything.

Check the opening, transitions and final action in the review page. Keep the
human's budget and all three checks intact. An unattended planner stops at plan
review. A model catalog entry is not proof that an executable plugin supports it.
